Who We Are

Built in Kigali, for East Africa

ConnectHive is a team building and corporate events company founded by a team of seven who understand what Rwandan workplaces actually look and feel like.

  • Our Mission

    To help organisations build stronger, happier teams through experiences that are genuinely fun and meaningfully impactful.

  • Our Vision

    To become the go-to team building brand in East Africa, known for transforming workplace culture one event at a time.

  • Our Difference

    Authentic energy, not scripted corporate vibes. We know Rwandan workplace culture — and we make every experience memorable because of it.
